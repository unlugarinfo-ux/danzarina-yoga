Dance

Dance classes

Dancing for the joy of movement, music and meeting others, at your own pace and with no competitive spirit.

The dances we teach

Tango

For adults. A dance of listening and connection, where you learn to walk together.

Salsa

For adults. A joyful, rhythmic dance to let go and share.

Dance for children

An introduction to and discovery of movement, music and expression.

How we teach

  • Open to everyone, beginners and experienced dancers alike.
  • Everyone progresses at their own pace: there is no performance to reach.
  • On your own or as a couple: you do not need to bring a partner.
  • Attention to listening to the body and to connection.
