Private lessons

Private dance and yoga lessons

A session designed for you: on your own, as a couple or in a small group, around a goal that suits you.

How it works

  1. A first conversation

    You tell us about yourself, what you would like and any constraints you may have.

  2. Your goal

    Opening the dance at a wedding, getting moving again, deepening a practice…

  3. A proposal

    We suggest a format: length, number of sessions, dance or yoga.

  4. The session

    Time at your own pace, focused on you.

  5. Follow-up

    We review together and adjust what comes next.

Place and arrangements

Possible venues, prices and travel arrangements are set out during the first conversation.

Let’s talk about your project

Describe what you have in mind in a few lines: we will get back to you to discuss it.
