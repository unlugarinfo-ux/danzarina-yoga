A journey in movement

A life of dancing and teaching

Danzarina grew out of the journey of Julieta, a movement artist from Argentina with over 20 years’ training in ballet, jazz, contemporary dance, tango, Argentine folk dance, salsa, zouk and kizomba. Today she combines dance and yoga in her own teaching method.

Today, Danzarina continues its path in Réunion Island.
