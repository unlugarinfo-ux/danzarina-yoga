About Danzarina

Danzarina was born of the wish to create a space devoted to exploring movement and spirituality through dance and yoga.

The founder

Julieta

Her story

A movement artist from Argentina, Julieta has been training in dance for over 20 years. A dancer of ballet, jazz, contemporary dance, tango, Argentine folk dance, salsa, zouk and kizomba, she has specialised in each of these styles.

She has had a long journey in dance and around the world, sharing her expertise in various cities and dance schools across Russia, Spain and France.

Her training

  • Over 20 years’ training in dance: ballet, jazz, contemporary dance, tango, Argentine folk dance, salsa, zouk and kizomba, among others
  • Training with renowned artists and teachers in Russia, Argentina (Córdoba, Buenos Aires), Spain and France
  • 200-hour Shantyoga certification (2019), training recognised by Yoga Alliance
  • Currently taking Iyengar yoga courses

Her path in yoga

Today she continues her path in yoga, creating her own teaching system in which she applies everything she has learnt over a life devoted to movement. She teaches dance to children, tango and salsa to adults, and yoga.

A journey in movement

Dancing as a couple

Julieta shares dance with Christian, a teacher and dancer, with whom she dances tango and salsa. Christian learnt African dance with Hamed.

Danzarina’s vision

One land, the same movement

Simplicity, nature, peace and fraternity: Danzarina wants to offer a space where everyone can move, breathe and connect, whatever their age or level. After a journey in movement, Danzarina now continues its work in Saint-Joseph, in the south of Réunion Island.
