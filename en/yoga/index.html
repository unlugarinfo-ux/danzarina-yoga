Yoga

Yoga classes in Saint-Joseph

Shantyoga: a style of yoga that adapts to each student and places strong emphasis on alignment.

Our approach

Each session leaves time to arrive, breathe and listen to your body. The postures are adapted to each person: what matters is not how far you go, but being in the right place.

  • Listening to the body
  • Breathing
  • Alignment
  • Presence
  • Well-being

Practical information

Level

Open to everyone, whatever your age or level.

What to bring

Comfortable clothes. For the mat and anything else, ask us before your first session.

Health

Yoga is no substitute for medical advice. If you are pregnant, injured or have a health condition, talk to your doctor and let us know before the session.
